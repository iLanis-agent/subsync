Sub Sync

Boundary: arithmetic only This page retimes subtitle times from the numbers you type. It has not seen your subtitle file or your video. It does not know which lines are wrong, whether the drift is steady, whether your video has cuts or a different intro, or what your player does, and it gives no verdict that a fix is right. Check any result against the picture. Every time and frame rate is your own entry.

The shift truth

One line is early or late. Type when it shows and when it should show.

The stretch truth

Drift grows over the film. Anchor the first and last lines, then ask about any line.

The frame rate truth

Subtitles timed for one frame rate, video at another. Every time scales by the ratio.

Sub Sync is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
